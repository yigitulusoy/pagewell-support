Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Pagewell is provided by Yiğit Ulusoy in Türkiye. Contact us about privacy at ulusoyyigit@hotmail.com.

Your reading, on your device

You can use Pagewell's library, reading plans, timer, and logging without creating an account. Your books, reading progress, goals, ratings, and highlights are stored on your device. Some optional features send the information described below to service providers. Device backups you enable through Apple are subject to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Information used by online features

Book searches and covers

Book searches send your search text to our catalogue service, hosted on Supabase, and to Open Library when needed. Book covers may load from Open Library. These services receive network information such as your IP address. Open Library may retain search queries in service logs and use them to improve search. Public book metadata may be cached in our shared catalogue; this is not a public record of your personal shelf.

Optional account and cloud backup

If you choose to sign in with Apple and use cloud backup, we receive your Apple account identifier and the email address Apple provides, which can be a private relay address. Supabase stores your account and synced library, reading sessions, daily logs, goals, plans, ratings, and book notes so they can be restored or synced across your devices. Highlight photos are stored locally and are not part of this app-managed cloud sync. We do not receive your Apple password.

Optional AI features

Pagewell uses DeepSeek through our server, hosted on Supabase, for AI conversations and book refreshers. When you send a chat message, your message and recent conversation are sent to this service. Reading-plan conversations may also send your selected reading purpose and aggregate counts of books and reading streaks. AI book discovery uses the preferences you choose to describe. A “Where was I?” refresher sends the selected book's title, author, current page, and time since you last read it. Do not enter information you do not want processed by this service.

Requests also use a separate random installation identifier and network information to enforce service limits and prevent abuse. For paid AI features, a signed Apple transaction is sent to our server to verify access. AI features are optional: you can create a reading plan and use the core app without AI.

DeepSeek processes data in China. Its handling of service data is described in DeepSeek's privacy policy. Our server does not intentionally maintain a conversation archive, but service providers may retain operational or security logs under their policies.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics are off until you allow them. If enabled, Pagewell sends feature interactions, screen activity, session timing, and limited technical event categories to Google Analytics 4 using a random installation identifier. These events can include starting or completing a Plus purchase and the selected subscription duration, but not payment-card details. Our analytics events do not include your book titles, authors, highlights, personal notes, ratings, or page and minute totals. Google receives network information when these requests are made. We do not send an advertising identifier or use this information for cross-app advertising tracking. You can turn analytics off in Settings; pending events are discarded and new events stop being sent.

Purchases

Apple handles subscription billing. We receive transaction and entitlement information to provide Pagewell Plus and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Photos, notifications, and sharing

A photo you select for a highlight stays in the app's local storage. Pagewell does not need access to your entire photo library to receive a photo you select in Apple's picker. Reading reminders use Apple's local notification service after permission. If you share a reading card or export data, you choose the destination through the system share sheet; that destination's privacy practices then apply.

Why we use information

We use information to provide the features you request, maintain accounts and backups, verify subscriptions, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and—with your permission—understand app usage. Where data-protection law applies, we rely on providing the requested service, consent for optional analytics and permissions, and legitimate interests in service security, subject to your applicable rights.

Service providers and international processing

Relevant providers include Apple, Supabase, Google Analytics, Open Library (Internet Archive), and DeepSeek. Their infrastructure may process information outside Türkiye or your country, including the United States and China. We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising. See the privacy policies of Apple, Supabase, Google, and Internet Archive for more about their services.

Retention and deletion

Local reading information remains until you delete it or remove the app, subject to any device backups. Cloud account data is retained to provide your account and sync until you delete the account or request deletion. Analytics and security records are retained only as needed for their stated purposes and the applicable provider settings or legal requirements. Contact us to request information about or deletion of data we control. Purchase records may need to be retained for refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

You can delete your account in Pagewell under Settings → Account → Delete account. This requests deletion of your server account and synced data. Your local reading library is kept. Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage that separately in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Your choices and rights

You can use the core app without an account, avoid optional AI features, disable analytics, and change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Depending on your jurisdiction, including applicable KVKK or GDPR rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to processing, or withdraw consent. You can also contact the relevant data-protection authority. Send requests to ulusoyyigit@hotmail.com; we may need to verify that the request concerns your data.

Children

Pagewell is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an online feature, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or our data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this page as appropriate.
