Pagewell support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with the app? Email ulusoyyigit@hotmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, Pagewell version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Subscriptions and purchases

Restore an existing purchase from the Pagewell Plus screen. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pagewell. Apple handles billing and refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your reading data

Your main reading library is stored on your device. Optional account backup and sync are available in the app. Highlights and photos are local, so keep a device backup before replacing or resetting your phone.

Delete an account or make a privacy request

In Pagewell, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes the server account and synced data while keeping the local library. It does not cancel an Apple subscription. For help or another privacy request, email us at the address above.

Book searches and AI

Online searches can take time. Check your connection and try the title together with the author's name. AI features are optional and may return incomplete or inaccurate information; please contact us if a result seems wrong.
